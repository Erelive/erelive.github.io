Android · Oct 2026 – Present

KuroShiro

An Android comic and novel reader. My fork of the open-source DropSauce, with a guide to what happens next that shows its sources.

Read the case study →

Problem
When a comic adapts a novel, finding out what happens next means trusting a summary with no sources, and guessing which novel chapter you are on.
What I built
  • A guide that finds the source novel through the MangaUpdates and AniList APIs, maps your chapter to a range of novel chapters, and checks every quoted fact in a Claude-written summary against its source.
  • A leaner reader: no whole-chapter preload on mobile data, one chapter-list scan per chapter instead of one per page turn, and a screen that is allowed to sleep.
  • Chapter comments that are loaded before you tap, offline download-ahead that cleans up after itself, and 247 new unit tests.

Windows · Aug 2026 – Present

Subordinate

Live captions for any audio playing on a Windows PC, transcribed on the machine's own graphics card.

Source on GitHub ↗

Measured on an RTX 4060 laptop over 100 seconds of continuous speech.

Problem
Live captions usually mean sending audio to a server, and the text keeps rewriting itself while the model changes its mind.
What I built
  • Runs OpenAI's Whisper (large-v3-turbo) through faster-whisper on CUDA, fast enough that captions trail speech by 0.64 s on average.
  • Locks a word in only once two passes agree. Confirmed text is drawn normally and the still-changing tail is dimmed, so captions stop visibly rewriting themselves.
  • Captures audio straight off the output device, skips silent stretches, and never touches the network.

  • Python
  • faster-whisper
  • CUDA
  • PyQt6

Windows · Jun – Aug 2026

Tengai

Turns any image into an OptiFine custom-sky pack for Minecraft: one picture wrapped across all six faces of the sky.

tengai.pages.dev ↗

Problem
By hand, a custom sky means cropping an image into six faces, mapping each one and packaging the files.
What I built
  • Splits one image across the six faces and writes the finished pack in about a second, entirely on the user's machine: no account, no upload, no command line.
  • Ships as a single 1.4 MB .exe, a C# shell around WebView2 that carries the whole app as one HTML file.
  • Gated the download with a Cloudflare Pages Worker that checks access keys on the server and signs sessions with HMAC-SHA-256, covered by 21 tests for refusal, tampering, expiry and open redirects.
Stack
  • JavaScript
  • HTML Canvas
  • C#
  • WebView2
  • Cloudflare Pages
