About

Security-minded engineering

I graduated from the University at Buffalo in 2025 with a B.S. in Computer Science, with coursework concentrated in software security, computer security, embedded operating systems, systems programming, and modern network concepts.

My work has pulled from both sides of that. I've shipped product features on a real-time collaborative app, and I've spent semesters running SYN floods and session hijacking attacks in a lab to understand exactly why protocols fail. Those two habits reinforce each other: knowing how things break makes you build them more carefully.

Most recently I worked as an AI Trainer through the Handshake AI Fellowship, evaluating model output against rubrics and writing adversarial prompts.
