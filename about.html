About Me!

Let’s get to know me a bit better. I’m a 22-year-old Computer Science graduate from Brooklyn, New York, with a strong interest in both technology and cybersecurity.

Outside of building and learning, I enjoy playing video games that emphasize strategy, world-building, and complex systems such as, Where Winds Meet, Baldur’s Gate 3, and Wuthering Waves. I enjoy games that reward critical thinking and adaptability, which often parallels how I approach technical problems.

I also make it a habit to stay current with developments in cybersecurity. I regularly browse cybersecurity-focused subreddits and Reddit communities to keep up with emerging threats, vulnerabilities, industry discussions, and real-world incidents. This helps me stay informed on how security concepts apply beyond textbooks and into practical, real-world scenarios.
